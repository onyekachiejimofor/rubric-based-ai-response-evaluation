## P01 — Return eligibility

Change-of-mind return requests must be made within 30 calendar days after delivery. The item must be unused, in its original condition and packaging, and the customer must provide the order number. A request is reviewed; approval is not automatic.

## P02 — How to request a return

To start an eligible return, email care@northstarhome.example and include the order number and the item to be returned. The support team will review eligibility and provide instructions. Do not send items to the headquarters without authorization.

## P03 — Refund processing

Once a refund is approved, allow 5–7 business days for processing. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. An exact bank posting date cannot be promised.

## P04 — Customer support

The only official customer-support email is care@northstarhome.example. Email support is available Monday–Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 West Africa Time (WAT, UTC+1). The company does not publish a phone-support number.

## P05 — Headquarters

Northstar Home headquarters: Suite 202, 18 Palm Grove Crescent, Independence Layout, Enugu, Enugu State, Nigeria. This is a fictional training address. The headquarters is an administrative office and does not accept walk-in returns.

## P06 — Standard delivery

Standard domestic delivery is estimated at 3–5 business days after order confirmation. It is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival date. Northstar Home does not guarantee next-day delivery.

## P07 — Order tracking

A tracking link is emailed to the customer when the order is dispatched. If it is missing after dispatch, the customer should check their email, including spam, then contact care@northstarhome.example with their order number.

## P08 — Order cancellation

Customers may request order cancellation before dispatch by emailing care@northstarhome.example with their order number. Orders cannot be cancelled after dispatch; eligible items may be considered for return after delivery.

## P09 — Delivery address changes

Delivery-address changes can be requested by emailing care@northstarhome.example with the order number before dispatch. Changes are not guaranteed after dispatch.

## P10 — Damaged or incorrect items

A damaged-item or wrong-item claim must be reported within 48 hours of delivery by emailing care@northstarhome.example with the order number and clear photos. Confirmed wrong-item or damaged-item cases are eligible for a company-paid return and remedy after review. Late reports are not guaranteed approval.

## P11 — Return shipping charges

For an eligible change-of-mind return, the customer pays the return-shipping charge. For a confirmed damaged or wrong item, Northstar Home pays the return-shipping charge.

## P12 — Exchanges

Northstar Home does not offer direct product exchanges. A customer seeking another color or model should request an eligible return and place a separate new order.

## P13 — Accepted payments

Northstar Home accepts Visa and Mastercard debit/credit cards and bank transfer. Cash on delivery is not available.

## P14 — Support and returns at headquarters

The Enugu headquarters is an administrative office, not a customer-service counter or returns drop-off point. Customers must first contact care@northstarhome.example for support or returns instructions.

## Policy precedence and limitations

- A detailed, specific policy takes priority over a general statement (e.g., damage/wrong-item claims under P10 vs change-of-mind eligibility under P01).
- Delivery estimates are not guarantees, and a customer should not be promised an outcome not supported by the policy.
- An AI response can be factually correct yet incomplete (such as stating only the headquarters city when asked where the office is).
- The original dataset did not provide the correct email or full fictional street address; these were **newly authored for version 2**. They are not real-world independently verified facts.
- A claimed citation to a "published policy" in the old benchmark is not evidence of a publicly existing policy.
